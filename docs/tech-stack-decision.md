# SportHub Technical Architecture Decision

## Executive Summary
After evaluating 3 options, **Option B (MERN Stack)** was selected for SportHub. This decision balances development speed, industry relevance, and long-term maintainability.

## Decision Matrix

| Criteria | Weight | Option A (Firebase) | Option B (MERN) | Option C (React Native) |
|----------|--------|---------------------|-----------------|------------------------|
| Development Speed | 20% | 9 | 7 | 6 |
| Learning Value | 25% | 6 | 9 | 8 |
| Control/Customization | 20% | 5 | 9 | 7 |
| Cost (Free Tier) | 15% | 8 | 9 | 9 |
| Mobile Support | 10% | 6 | 5 | 10 |
| Community Support | 10% | 8 | 10 | 8 |
| **Weighted Score** | **100%** | **7.0** | **8.2** | **7.8** |
